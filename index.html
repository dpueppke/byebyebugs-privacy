Privacy Policy

Bye Bye Bugs: Bug Zapper

Effective November 8, 2026

Game data

Bye Bye Bugs: Bug Zapper does not require an account. The game does not collect personal information or send your gameplay activity to us. It does not contain ads, analytics, or tracking tools.

The game saves your progress, earned zap colors, and preferences such as sound and haptics on your device. It uses this information to resume your game and remember your settings. The game does not send these saves to us or sync them between devices.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Unlock Full Game purchase through the App Store. The game uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify whether the purchase is active and to restore it when requested. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under its Privacy Policy.

Deleting game data

To remove the game’s local save and settings from your device, choose Delete App in iOS. Deleting the app does not delete your purchase history with Apple.
